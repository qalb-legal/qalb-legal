Common questions

  • How do I restore my subscription on a new phone? Open Settings in Qalb and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.
  • How do I cancel my subscription? On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Qalb. Subscriptions are managed by Apple.
  • The prayer times are wrong. Make sure Qalb is allowed to use your location (iPhone Settings, Qalb, Location), then open the app again so it can recalculate for where you are.
  • The Qibla compass is not accurate. Move away from metal objects and electronics, and move your phone in a figure-of-eight to calibrate the compass.
  • I want my post or comment removed. Use the Delete option on your own post or comment, or email us and we will remove it.
  • I saw something inappropriate. Use Report on the post or comment, or Block to hide everything from that person. You can also email us.
  • I found an error in a dua, hadith or translation. Please email us with the item's title and what should be corrected, so we can check and fix it.
