Privacy Policy — Qalb

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Qalb ("the App") is built around a simple principle: we collect the minimum needed to make the App work, nothing is sold to anyone, and nothing is used to advertise to you. There is no sign-up and no account. This page explains exactly what the App does with your information.

What stays on your phone

  • Location (optional): if you allow it, your location is used to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. This is calculated on your phone. Your location is never sent to us or stored by us.
  • Camera (optional): the camera is used for three things, all processed on your phone: showing the Qibla direction in the AR view; reading your pulse from your fingertip for the calm check; and, if you turn on the Fajr wake-up alarm, recognising your prayer mat or sink so you can switch the alarm off. Camera images are never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. On Android, the recognising is done by Google's ML Kit, which runs on your phone; Google may receive anonymous statistics about how well it performs, but never the images.
  • Calm check results: your heart rate and heart rhythm readings are calculated and kept on your phone only. The calm check is a reflection tool, not a medical device.
  • Your settings and progress: your language, reminders, alarm settings, tasbih counts, game progress and the list of people you have blocked are saved on your phone.

What is sent from your phone, and why

  • AI assistant chat: before your first message, the App asks for your permission. If you agree, your messages are sent to our server and to Anthropic, the AI company that powers the assistant, so it can write replies. We do not save your conversations. They are not linked to your name and are not used for advertising. Anthropic processes them to produce the replies under its commercial terms, which do not allow them to be used to train its AI models. To limit how many messages can be sent each day, our server keeps a daily count for a random ID created by the App. This ID is not linked to you.
  • Community posts and comments: if you choose to post or comment, your text and the display name you choose (a nickname is fine) are stored on our backend, hosted by Supabase, and shown to other users of the App. Before it is published, the text is checked by an automated moderation system, which sends it to Anthropic for that check only.
  • Reports and likes: when you report or like a post or comment, that action is recorded on our backend without any personal details.
  • Subscriptions: purchases are handled by Apple (on iPhone) or Google Play (on Android), and by our subscription service, RevenueCat. RevenueCat gives your purchases a random ID so they can be restored. We never see your payment details; Apple or Google handles payment.
  • App content: duas, stories, audio and images are downloaded from our content service, Sanity. Like any website, these services can see the network address your phone connects from, but we do not use it to identify you.

What we don't do

  • No advertising networks or advertising IDs are used anywhere in the App.
  • No analytics or tracking tools are built into the App, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone for marketing.
  • We do not collect your real name, email address or phone number.

Content moderation

Community posts and comments are checked by an automated moderation system before they are published, to keep out harmful or inappropriate content. You can report any post or comment and block any person, and their posts and comments will no longer appear for you. Reported content is reviewed and removed if it breaks our community guidelines.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • Your posts and comments are kept until you delete them. You can delete your own post or comment at any time with the in-app Delete option, or email us and we will remove it.
  • Your AI chat messages are not saved by us.
  • Everything saved on your phone is removed when you delete the App.

Children

The App is made for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
